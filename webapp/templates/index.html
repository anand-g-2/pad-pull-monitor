How to draw the ROI

  1. Wait until the belt is visible in the live preview (ideally with a batch of pads at the sensor).
  2. Click Capture frame to draw ROI.
  3. Click around the belt area to place corners. Drag a corner to move it, right-click a corner to remove it.
  4. Include the whole pad lane and the staging spot at the sensor. Leave out the oven shadow and the pulling unit's parking area.
  5. Click Save ROI.
